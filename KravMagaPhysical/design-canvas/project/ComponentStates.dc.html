Rebalance motion
  1. The edited card locks at once: no animation, it follows your finger.
  2. Auto cards that moved get a cyan wash that fades over 1.1 s, and their value and points tick up into place (350 ms spring).
  3. Slider fills, thumbs and split bars spring to their new length (340 ms, slight overshoot).
  4. A chip says how much easier or harder each Auto event got, then clears after 1.4 s.
  5. Reduced motion: no springs or ticks. Values change in place and the wash is a plain fade.
